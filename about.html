His story

Ancient wisdom. Modern tools. One purpose.

Jitendra Khimlani is a senior Art of Living faculty, motivational speaker, NLP trainer, Bach remedy therapist, intuition trainer and life transformation coach based in Vadodara, Gujarat.

For more than twelve years he has conducted workshops for youth, corporates, children and adults. Over 2,00,000 people have attended his workshops, sessions and seminars at universities, institutes, companies and government organisations including the Police, the Army and the NDRF.

His gift is helping young people connect with their true potential — removing doubt and insecurity, instilling self-confidence, and giving them the clarity to identify their purpose and build the skills to pursue it. He also offers branding ideas and guidance to entrepreneurs and start-ups.

The journey

Milestones of service.

The start

Called to teach

Began sharing Art of Living programs with youth in Vadodara — the beginning of a journey that would touch lakhs of lives.

2014

#SevaTrend is born

Conceptualised #SevaTrend — a movement to turn sparks of kindness into a fire of positive change through seva and random acts of kindness.

2020

Colors of Life & Covid relief

Launched "Colors of Life", an online series to spread smiles and motivation during the pandemic, and distributed 1,000+ food grain kits to daily wage earners with the Vadodara District Collector.

Ongoing
