FAQs

I want to take this course, but…

Who can join this program?

Anyone aged 18 and above — students, working professionals, homemakers or entrepreneurs. You don't need prior experience with meditation or yoga; the program is designed for beginners and experienced practitioners alike.

How does it help with stress and health?

Research shows that 70–90% of illnesses are linked to stress. Studies on Sudarshan Kriya show cortisol (the stress hormone) drops within weeks of practice, along with improvements in blood pressure, heart rate variability and sleep. By calming the mind, you help prevent stress-triggered conditions before they become chronic.

Will I feel results immediately?

Most participants report feeling lighter, calmer and more energetic from the very first session. With regular practice the benefits multiply — better sleep, less stress and more emotional balance.

I've tried meditation before and couldn't focus. Will this be different?

Yes. The guided techniques use specific breathing patterns that naturally quiet the mind, so you don't have to "force" focus. Even people who struggled with meditation before find this approach easy and enjoyable.

Are there any side-effects?

An undying smile is the only side-effect! Sudarshan Kriya™ is practised by millions of people every day. If you have a history of asthma, high blood pressure, heart problems or back pain, let your teacher know beforehand and you will be guided differently during the session.

Why is there a fee?

First, to make sure you commit your time to the workshop. Second, your contribution funds many Art of Living service projects in India — such as sending 70,000 tribal children to school, reviving 43 rivers, empowering 2,04,802 rural youth with livelihood skills and lighting 720 villages with solar lamps.

I have no stress. Why should I still join?

Great — you're living the best life! But we don't start saving only when we run out of money, or start exercising only after losing our health. This builds inner reserves of resilience and strength for the times you might need them.
