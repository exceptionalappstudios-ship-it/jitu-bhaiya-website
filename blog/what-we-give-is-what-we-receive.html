What We Give Is What We Ultimately Receive

The philosophy behind #SevaTrend and why one act of kindness can start a revolution.

In 2014, a simple question would not leave me alone: we have trends for fashion, for music, for dance challenges — why not a trend for kindness?

That question became #SevaTrend.

Sparks into fire

Every act of seva — selfless service — is a spark. One person helping another may seem small. But sparks, gathered together, become a fire. And a fire, once lit, spreads on its own. The aim was never to do seva alone; it was to inspire thousands to do it with us.

“What we give is what we ultimately receive. Give joy, and joy finds its way back to you.”

Why service heals the giver

When we serve, something shifts inside. Our own problems shrink to their true size. We feel connected, useful, alive. Many of the young people I work with discovered their confidence not in a classroom, but while handing a gift to a child in a tribal village or packing a food kit for a family in need.

How you can join

  • Do one random act of kindness today — and share it to inspire someone else.
  • Volunteer for a seva drive in your city.
  • Bring your friends, colleagues or classmates into the movement.

You don't need to wait for a big moment to make a difference. Be the spark. Be the revolution.
