1,000+ Food Grain Kits Donated to Daily Wage Earners

How the community came together during the lockdown to make sure no family went hungry.

When the Covid-19 lockdown began, the people hit hardest were those who earn day by day — construction workers, vendors, labourers. Overnight, their income stopped. Their hunger did not.

A community responds

Through the spirit of #SevaTrend and the generosity of many supporters, more than 1,000 food grain kits were prepared and donated to daily wage earners, handed over through the office of the Vadodara District Collector so they reached the families who needed them most.

“In difficult times, seva is not charity. It is simply family taking care of family.”

Thank you

To every donor, volunteer and well-wisher who made this possible — thank you. You proved that when we stand together, no one has to stand alone.

If you would like to support or volunteer for future seva drives, please get in touch.
