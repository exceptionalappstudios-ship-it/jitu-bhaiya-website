About me

A life devoted to lighting up other lives.

Mr. Jitendra Khimlani — lovingly called Jitu Bhaiya — is a senior Art of Living faculty, motivational speaker, NLP trainer, Bach remedy therapist, intuition trainer and life transformation coach.

For the past 12 years he has conducted workshops for youth, corporates, children and adults. Till date, more than 2,00,000 people have attended his workshops, sessions and seminars at universities, institutes, companies and government organisations like the Police, the Army and the NDRF. He also provides branding ideas and guidance to entrepreneurs and start-ups.
