The movement

#SevaTrend

In 2014, Jitu Bhaiya conceptualised #SevaTrend — a simple yet audacious idea: what if kindness became the trend?

The goal is to reach the masses and bring positive change to their lives and the lives of the people around them — through seva, through random acts of kindness, and by inspiring the world one small action at a time.

Every workshop, every volunteer and every shared smile adds fuel to that fire.
