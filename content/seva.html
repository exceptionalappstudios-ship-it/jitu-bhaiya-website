Social initiatives

Kindness, multiplied.

For more than 10 years, Team Happiness — led by Jitu Bhaiya — has turned small sparks of seva into a movement across Vadodara.

The movement

#SevaTrend — Neki Kar, Social Media Par Daal.

A unique revolution conceptualised by Jitendra Khimlani, inspiring 100,000+ people every year to do more seva.

Do a good deed — and share it, so that your kindness inspires someone else's. The goal is to convert sparks into a big fire: to reach the masses and bring positive change to their lives and the lives of the people around them.
