Relief in the Heat: 1,000+ Litres of Chhas Across Vadodara

Team Happiness served over 1,000 litres of fresh chhas to outdoor workers during Vadodara's hottest days.

March in Vadodara brings some of the hottest days of the year. For thousands of people — especially outdoor workers, commuters and daily-wage earners — the heat becomes more than discomfort; it becomes a genuine health risk.

Recognising this need, Art of Living – Team Happiness organised a large-scale chhas distribution drive under the #SevaTrend initiative. Over 1,000 litres of fresh chhas were distributed across multiple locations in Vadodara, bringing hydration, comfort and much-needed respite.

A timely intervention

Rising temperatures hit hardest those who spend long hours outdoors: construction workers, street vendors, rickshaw drivers, security guards and sanitation staff. Volunteers set up distribution points across high-exposure areas so anyone passing by could stop for a refreshing glass.

A simple drink with significant impact

  • Immediate hydration and cooling relief
  • Prevention of heat fatigue
  • A moment of rest — and a sense of being cared for

The reactions — a nod of gratitude, a heartfelt thank-you, a visible sigh of relief — showed how meaningful small acts of service can be.

Organised and heartfelt

A city-wide drive of this scale took coordinated effort: preparing more than 1,000 litres of hygienic chhas, placing distribution points, continuous refills, and cleanliness and safety at every location. Whether serving commuters at busy intersections or areas with heavy foot traffic, volunteers offered relief with warmth and respect.

Serving society with compassion, awareness and timely action.
Team Happiness · led by Jitendra Khimlani
