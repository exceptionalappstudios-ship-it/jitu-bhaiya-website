Little Feet, Bright Futures: 500+ Shoes Distributed Across Multiple Locations

Over 500 pairs of new school shoes — personally fitted — for underprivileged children.

For many underprivileged children, the simple act of walking to school is a daily challenge. Uneven roads, harsh weather and the lack of proper footwear often make education physically difficult.

Recognising this need, Art of Living – Team Happiness initiated a large-scale school shoe distribution drive across multiple locations — so that children not only attend school comfortably but also walk forward with confidence and dignity. In total, over 500 pairs of new school shoes were distributed, along with cupcakes and snacks.

A mission rooted in service

The initiative was inspired by the vision of Gurudev Sri Sri Ravi Shankar, who emphasises that service to society is the foundation of a compassionate and harmonious community. With this spirit, volunteers reached several villages and communities to create real, practical impact for the children who need it most.

Beyond distribution

Volunteers personally measured and checked each student's shoe size to guarantee the correct fit — an important detail often overlooked in large donation drives. Alongside the shoes, the team organised interactive games and short learning activities, turning an ordinary day into an unforgettable one.

Impact that goes beyond footwear

  • Protects young feet from injuries, infections and harsh weather
  • Encourages regular school attendance
  • Builds a sense of dignity and confidence
  • Removes one economic burden from low-income families
Meaningful change often begins with small, consistent acts of care.

This project would not have been possible without the generosity of donors and the dedicated involvement of volunteers. Thank you for helping hundreds of children walk a little more comfortably towards their dreams.
