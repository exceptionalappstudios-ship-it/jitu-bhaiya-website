Khushiyo Ka Tohfa: Spreading Light in the Tribal Villages of Dediapada

1,000+ Diwali goodie bags for children in the remote tribal villages of Dediapada.

Diwali — the festival of lights — is often associated with shimmering lamps, delicious sweets, vibrant rangolis and joyous celebrations. But for thousands of children living in the remote tribal belts of Gujarat, Diwali often passes quietly: without festivities, without gifts, and sometimes without even the feeling of being seen or remembered.

This year, Art of Living – Team Happiness set out to change that.

On 17th October, volunteers travelled deep into the tribal villages of Dediapada and other nearby areas around Vadodara, carrying not just bags of sweets and stationery, but a mission — to bring happiness, dignity and a sense of belonging to children who rarely experience festive joy.

A journey beyond celebrations

The trip to Dediapada wasn't just a distribution drive; it was a journey into lives that often remain unseen. Narrow forest paths, scattered mud homes, vast stretches of silence interrupted only by children's laughter — each moment reminded volunteers why this seva matters.

These children, living far from urban privileges, don't often get to celebrate Diwali the way most of us do. So this year, Team Happiness decided to take Diwali to them.

What was inside the goodie bags?

  • 🍬 Assorted sweets
  • 🧨 Safe crackers
  • ✏️ Stationery and drawing materials
  • 🎨 Colours to ignite their creativity

But beyond the items, what they truly received was a feeling of being loved. Their eyes sparkled as they held the bags close. Some opened them instantly with excitement; others waited patiently to share the moment with their siblings.

The real gift: joy

As volunteers handed out each goodie bag, they witnessed something priceless — the pure, unfiltered joy of a child who feels seen. One child hugged his bag like a treasure. Another couldn't stop smiling at a small box of crayons. A little girl whispered, “Yeh sab mere liye hai?” — her disbelief turning into happiness.

“When kindness shines, the whole world becomes brighter.”

A collective effort of love

This initiative was possible only because of the generosity of donors and the dedication of countless volunteers who spent hours packing, organising and travelling. More than 1,000 goodie bags were prepared with love — and distributed with even more love.

Why this seva matters

  • A simple gift becomes a celebration
  • A notebook becomes an opportunity
  • A packet of colours becomes a dream
  • A little gesture becomes a memory they carry forever

To every donor, volunteer and supporter — thank you. Your kindness travelled miles, crossed forests and touched thousands of little hearts. This is not the end, only the beginning.
