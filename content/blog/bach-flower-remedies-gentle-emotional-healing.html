Bach Flower Remedies: Gentle Emotional Healing

How 38 flower essences offer natural support for stress, fear and emotional overwhelm.

In the 1930s, Dr. Edward Bach, a British physician, became convinced that our emotional state plays a profound role in our wellbeing. He developed a system of 38 flower essences, each addressing a particular emotional pattern — fear, uncertainty, loneliness, over-sensitivity, despair and more.

How it works

Bach flower therapy is gentle and natural. In a consultation, we talk about what you are going through — not just the symptoms, but the feelings beneath them. Based on this, a personalised blend of remedies is prepared for you to take a few drops at a time through the day.

“Treat the person, not the problem. When the emotions find balance, life begins to flow again.”

Common reasons people come

  • Stress, anxiety and racing thoughts
  • Fear before exams, interviews or big decisions
  • Grief, loss and difficult life transitions
  • Low confidence, guilt or feeling stuck

A complement, not a replacement

Bach remedies are safe for all ages and work beautifully alongside meditation and breathwork. They are a complementary support for emotional wellbeing and are not a substitute for medical treatment — if you have a medical condition, please continue to consult your doctor.

Sometimes all we need is a gentle nudge back towards balance. Nature, as always, has one waiting.
