1,000+ Food Grain Kits Donated to Daily Wage Earners

Handed over to Vadodara District Collector IAS Shalini Agrawal for distribution to daily wage earners.

When the lockdown stopped the daily income of thousands of workers, Team Happiness came together to make sure families did not go hungry.

More than 1,000 food grain kits were handed over to Vadodara District Collector IAS Shalini Agrawal, to be distributed to needy daily wage earners by the VMC.

In difficult times, seva is simply family taking care of family.
