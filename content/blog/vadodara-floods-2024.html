Vadodara Floods 2024: Standing Strong in Times of Trouble

200+ Team Happiness volunteers brought tea, water and food to families caught in the floods.

The city of Baroda was badly hit by heavy rains in the last week of August 2024. Vadodara experienced a flood-like situation as the Vishwamitri River's water level surged to 35 feet, according to authorities.

Our volunteers — “Team Happiness”, led by our beloved Jitu Bhaiya — rose like warriors in this situation. They were ready to help the residents caught in the floods, distributing tea, water and food packets.

The team of more than 200 people covered areas like Chhani, Manjalpur, Gotri Circle and Vadodara Railway Station. Many food packets and water bottles reached families in need.

In times of crisis, seva is how a community holds itself together.

After a severe flood, acts of seva play a crucial role in easing suffering and aiding recovery. Team Happiness, led by Jitendra Khimlani, has been doing such seva projects for more than 10 years.
