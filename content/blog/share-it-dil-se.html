Share It… Dil Se: A Judgment-Free Space for Honest Conversations

Conceptualised by Jitendra Khimlani — 150+ people, 25 Happiness Coaches and a café where everyone was heard.

In a world that's always talking, one human need often goes unheard — the need to express. To speak. To be understood. To feel truly listened to.

With this intention, Art of Living Vadodara hosted a one-of-a-kind emotional wellness event, “Share It… Dil Se”, on 28–29 March 2025 at Artistry Café, Sevasi–Sindhrot Road. It offered something rare: a warm cup of coffee, a welcoming environment, and a judgment-free space to share whatever lies heavy — or hopeful — in the heart.

A concept born from compassion

“Share It… Dil Se” was envisioned and conceptualised by Jitendra Khimlani, whose mission was to create a safe emotional haven for people of all ages. His idea was simple yet powerful: walk in, sit down, sip coffee — and speak freely, without judgment, interruption or the fear of being misunderstood.

150+ participants, one shared need

Over 150 people attended across two days, each with a different story. They spoke about personal challenges, relationships and family, stress and anxiety, work-life pressure, and their dreams, hopes and unspoken fears. Many left feeling lighter, more connected and deeply grateful.

25 Happiness Coaches. 50+ volunteers. One purpose.

Under Jitu Bhaiya's guidance, 25 trained Happiness Coaches offered compassionate presence and attentive listening, while 50+ volunteers created a warm, welcoming experience — soft music, comfortable corners and peaceful surroundings.

A space that became a sanctuary

People who walked in hesitantly ended up speaking wholeheartedly. Strangers connected through shared humanity. Many said they hadn't spoken this openly in years. Some shed tears — of relief, gratitude or realisation.

“Sometimes, what a person needs most is not advice — but a silent, compassionate listener.” — Jitendra Khimlani

A movement, not just an event

It started conversations that continue today, opened hearts that had long been closed, and reminded people that they are not alone. The overwhelming response has inspired Art of Living Vadodara to create more such emotional wellness spaces.
