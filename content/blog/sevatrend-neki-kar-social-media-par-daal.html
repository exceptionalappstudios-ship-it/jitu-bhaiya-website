#SevaTrend — Neki Kar, Social Media Par Daal

A unique revolution conceptualised by Jitendra Khimlani — inspiring 100,000+ people every year to do more seva.

#SevaTrend is a unique revolution conceptualised by Jitendra Khimlani, inspiring 100,000+ people every year to do more seva.

The idea is simple: Neki kar, social media par daal — do a good deed, and share it on social media. Not for applause, but so that one act of kindness inspires the next, and the next.

The aim is to convert sparks into a big fire — to reach the masses and bring positive change to their lives and the lives of people around them, through seva and random acts of kindness.

“What we give is what we ultimately receive.” — Jitendra Khimlani

From Diwali goodie bags and school shoes to chhas in the summer heat and relief during floods, every Team Happiness initiative carries the #SevaTrend spirit. Do one act of kindness today — and share it to inspire someone else.
